Bizzy's Learning Hive — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy applies to the Android app Bizzy's Learning Hive (also listed on Google Play as Spell Buzz: Kids Learning, package com.spellingbook.spellbuzz).

Short version: Bizzy's Learning Hive is made for children. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything a child does stays on the device.

Who we are

Bizzy's Learning Hive is made by Learn with Singam (Azhagiya Singam). It is a spelling, math and science course and game app for children from preschool to Grade 5. Contact: learnwithsingam@gmail.com.

Information stored on the device

  • The learner's first name (or nickname), grade and colour, chosen by a parent.
  • Learning progress: spelling, math and science lessons completed, answers used for the grown-ups report, game scores, word lists, and the spelling-bee date if set.
  • Word lists and voice packs that a parent adds.

This information is stored only on the device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting a learner (Grown-ups → Delete) or uninstalling the app erases it.

Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software.

Internet use

  • Definitions for pasted words (optional). When a parent pastes a school word list and a word is not in the app's built-in dictionary, the app may look the word up at dictionaryapi.dev. Only the word itself is sent — never a name or any other information.
  • Purchases. The optional one-time “Full Hive” purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment.

Children's privacy

Bizzy's Learning Hive is designed for children and follows Google Play's Families Policy, the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR. Because we collect no personal information, no parental consent for data collection is needed. Purchases and settings are behind a grown-ups check.

Microphone and other permissions

The app does not use the microphone, camera, location or contacts. A parent may choose a file (a voice pack) from the device; the app reads only that file.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
